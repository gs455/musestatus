v1.0.0 — 10/23/26

  • First release of musestatus at armanai.pages.dev/musestatus/: Muse's own status in 12 movable panes — Starred & pinned, File system, Preferences, Machines, Connectors, Usage & billing, Automations, Skills, Tools / namespaces, Assistants & chats, Artifacts & hub, and Memory.
  • Data is published by the VM (musestatus-publish) into KV and stamped per pane; the file-system pane browses per-folder chunks lazily and the Preferences pane loads file bodies on click.
  • Dense editable tables, floating panes window, edit mode, verbose toggle, spacing sliders, star toggles, and view state synced through KV — the claudestatus chrome, as a sibling app.
